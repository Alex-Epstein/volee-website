How It Works

FOUR STEPS TOCOMPETITION

Get on the court and climbing the ladder in minutes.

01

Build Your Profile

Tell us your ball level, play style, and home area. Get placed in the right tier automatically.

02

Find Your Ladder

See the 100 closest players in your tier. Browse open challenges nearby or post your own.

03

Challenge & Play

Pick a match format — Best of 3, Pro Set, or Fast4 — agree on a time and location, then play.

04

Report & Rise

Both players confirm the score. Your ladder position updates instantly. Win against higher seeds to climb fast.

Features

BUILT FOR THECOMPETITOR

Everything you need to find, schedule, and track competitive junior tennis — in one place.

🏆

Live Ladder Rankings

Your position updates the moment both players confirm the score. Beat someone ranked higher and you take their position — it's that direct. Separate boys and girls ladders, with full season stats and career records tracked automatically.

Elo-Based RatingsBoys & Girls LaddersSeason Resets
📍

Smart Challenges

Post an open challenge for anyone in your tier nearby to pick up. Or challenge a specific player directly. Set the location, format, and time — they accept, counter, or decline.

📋

3 Match Formats

Best of 3 Sets, Pro Set (8 games), or Fast4. Each format has its own rating weight — longer matches count for more.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Get push notifications when you're challenged, when someone accepts, when a match is about to start, and when it's time to report your score.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Accounts

Parents can manage multiple kids from one login. Switch between profiles easily and oversee match history across your whole household.

🌆

Multi-City Play

Travel to another city? Challenge players there for unranked matches. Your home city is where ranked matches count — everywhere else is practice.

The Ladder System

SIX TIERS.YOUR LEVEL.

Every player is placed in one of six tiers based on their ball level and play style. Cross-tier play is only allowed between adjacent tiers.

Within each tier, players are ranked Low, Mid, or High on the ladder. Beat a player above you and you take their position. Seasons reset every 4 months, with your new starting position based on where you finished.
